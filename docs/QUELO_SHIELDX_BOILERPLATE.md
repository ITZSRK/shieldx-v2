## Extended (deck appendix, detailed About page, incubator application)

> **The problem.** Collections in India is a multi-lakh-crore stressed-asset challenge run largely on dialers, spreadsheets, and intuition. Lenders execute millions of borrower contacts with no systematic layer deciding how each conversation should happen — and no record of why each decision was made or what worked.
>
> **What ShieldX is.** ShieldX is decisioning infrastructure: the layer between a lender's core systems and its communication rails. Its four products — **Decision**, **Engage**, **Assist**, and **Intelligence** — form one loop: the platform decides the treatment for every account, executes it across every channel and agency, supports agents before and during conversations, and analyzes what was actually said to inform the next decision.
>
> **Why it wins.** Models are commoditized; the moat is the loop. ShieldX is the only architecture that owns both the decision layer and the conversation layer of collections — so it is the only one where what a borrower says in a call systematically changes the next decision on that account. Every decision is logged, explainable, and auditable by design, aligned with RBI's emerging model risk management framework. And as ShieldX deploys across lenders, it accumulates anonymized, cohort-level treatment knowledge no single institution could build alone.
>
> **The company.** Quelo Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (Mumbai, incorporated 2024) is DPIIT-recognized. Founder and CEO Sudarson Radhakrishnan brings 18 years across Citibank, Standard Chartered, Armsoftech, and Yubi. ShieldX is live with a leading private-sector bank, with active engagements across ARCs and collection agencies.

## Usage rules

1. **Category words are fixed:** "decisioning infrastructure," "layer," "rails." Never "collections software," "AI company," "voice AI company," "SaaS tool."
2. **Client reference is always** "a leading private-sector bank." Never the name.
3. **No numbers** for model performance, AUC, accuracy, or production volumes anywhere.
4. AI is mentioned as method, never identity. Voice (Diya/VI) is a channel/input, never the lead.
5. Founder line is singular — sole founder. No other names in team references.
6. When a shorter and longer version both fit, use the shorter.
